## Night blood rose

Scaled for five level-2 characters. The published blood-rose bush is a CR 5 legendary plant (AC 15, 105 HP, hit-die drain, two legendary actions). This block is the table's own shorter version.

The bush looks like swamp roses until it moves. It wants blood, then it crawls to a new patch. The party needs sap taken **after it has already drunk**, because that is when their fog answers the plant and becomes something the druid can pull off.

Win by cutting one vine cube loose after the bush has drunk, or by destroying the heart. They do not have to clear the board. The ground under the starting cubes is difficult terrain.

Cubes are the body. Each cube fills one square. People cannot move through a cube, and a line of cubes between two people blocks help and touch. That is how the bush splits the party.

- Start with the heart cube and one vine cube snapped to it.
- On its turn it snaps on one new cube, adjacent to any cube already in play, up to six cubes. It places that cube in a gap so the party is split.
- The whole shape can crawl 15 feet. Cubes keep their places relative to the heart.
- Two vine attacks from any cube: +4 to hit, 5 (1d6+2) piercing, against a creature next to that cube.
- Grab: Medium or smaller, escape DC 12, up to two creatures.
- A vine cube is AC 13 and 8 HP. Removing it deals that damage to the heart as well. If a cube was the only link back to the heart, every cube past the break falls off the board.
- Heart: AC 13, 45 hit points. At 0 the bush dies and every cube drops.
- Drink (bonus action): each grabbed creature takes 4 (1d6+1) necrotic. The bush heals half. The fog on that creature becomes visible to the party for the first time.
- Mist (recharge 5–6): 10-foot heavy mist for 1 minute, or until a wind. Inside it, the next attack or Perception is at disadvantage. The bush loses 3 HP to vent the mist.

No legendary actions. No hit-die drain.

Harvest: pry off one vine cube after the bush has drunk this fight. That cube is the bloom. The sap plus the fog it just showed is the whole recipe.

## Chalice of Tomorrow

Wondrous relic, rare. This is the Act 1 necromancer reward. The party carries it from the shrine until the arrest. The guild then keeps it with the amulet. One strong heal, paid the next morning. Right for level 2 because the cup locks itself after a drink.

The published card is not copied here. Table version:

- Each dawn the cup fills with dark red liquid. The liquid disappears if it is poured into anything else.
- As an action, drink. Regain hit points equal to half your hit point maximum (round down).
- At the next dawn, your hit point maximum drops by that many hit points. Say it when they drink: the wound did not close, it moved to tomorrow.
- The drop lasts until they finish a long rest that begins after that dawn. They cannot drink again while the drop remains.
- Rhythm: drink today, pay at dawn, sleep the debt off the following night, drink again after that. About one use every two days.

A level-2 character with 16 hit points drinks for 8, then wakes the next day with a maximum of 8 until the night after. The guild locked the cup because the liquid looks like the curse. It does not lift the fog. The druid can say the cup and the rose drink from the same hunger.
