# Chalice of Tomorrow

### Header

**Name:** Chalice of Tomorrow
**Image:** ../../scenarios/when-the-lanterns-fall-silent/assets/items/zavtrashnia-chasha.jpg
**Rarity:** Artifact
**Type:** Wondrous item

### Properties

**Attunement:** Not required

### Description

Each dawn the chalice fills with dark red liquid. Poured into anything else, the liquid disappears.

As an action, drink. Regain hit points equal to half your hit point maximum, rounded down.

At the next dawn, your hit point maximum drops by that many hit points. The wound did not close. It moved to tomorrow.

The drop lasts until you finish a long rest that begins after that dawn. You cannot drink again while the drop remains.
